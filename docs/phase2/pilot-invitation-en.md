# Invitation — GCC Phase 2 Teacher Pilot

**Subject:** Invitation: Help test Global Classroom Connect with teachers worldwide

Dear colleagues,

We are inviting a small group of approximately **10 teachers from Japan and other countries** to help us test **Global Classroom Connect (GCC)** before we introduce it to the wider Global Classroom Community.

GCC is designed to make two things simple: **Create a Session** to invite another classroom, or **Find a Partner** and request to join an international exchange.

As a pilot teacher, we would love you to try the complete flow: register your teacher account, submit your school profile for verification, create or find a session, apply or approve a partner request, and tell us what was easy or confusing. We will also check how the site works on computers, iPads, and smartphones.

**GCC:** https://gcc.pic-speak-story.com/#/teacher-access

Participation is free. This is a small controlled trial, not a general public launch.

**Important for this test:** automatic join-request emails and built-in GCC chat are not yet available. Please check the notification/request section inside GCC and coordinate via your mutually agreed outside service (such as school email, WhatsApp, LINE or Messenger) with your pilot coordinator's help where necessary. Please do not put student names or private meeting links into public fields.

If you would like to help, please **reply privately** with your country, the school level you teach, whether you would prefer to host or join a session, and the device you plan to use. We will send each volunteer the short step-by-step pilot guide and coordinate participating schools.

Your feedback will help make GCC easier and safer for teachers worldwide.

Thank you for being part of the Global Classroom community!
